|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Notes

- Record prompts at a useful summary level; remove secrets and personal data.
- Distinguish generated output from changes the team accepted or edited.
- Record real build, lint, API, and manual checks. Never mark an unrun check as passed.
- Note limitations and rejected suggestions as well as successful assistance.
